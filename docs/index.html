What is N4V FOR WEBI? N4V FOR WEBI is a software suite published by Need4Viz, a Paris-based SAP Build Partner. It extends SAP BusinessObjects Web Intelligence (WebI) with advanced data visualization, cartography, artificial intelligence and web publishing, while keeping the existing BusinessObjects universes, security and reports.

Simplify

Build richer WebI documents with guided extension panels instead of scripting. Business users and report designers stay in the tool they already know.

Enhance

Add interactive charts, geographic maps and natural-language AI on top of the data already modelled in your BusinessObjects universes.

Facilitate

Publish WebI content as responsive HTML5 and share it widely, without additional SAP front-end licences.

The four modules

N4V Widgets

More than 60 interactive chart types for Web Intelligence: KPIs, advanced bar, line and flow charts, gauges, tables and more.

N4V Maps

Geographic information (GIS) and cartography inside WebI: choropleth, markers, layers and drill-down on your own data.

N4V Publisher

Export WebI documents to standalone responsive HTML5 dashboards that can be shared outside the BusinessObjects portal.

N4V Intelligence

Artificial intelligence for WebI: natural-language questions, data explanations and assisted analysis.

Frequently asked questions

What is N4V FOR WEBI?

A suite of extensions for SAP BusinessObjects Web Intelligence that adds advanced visualization, maps, AI and HTML5 export. It is published by Need4Viz.

Does it require migrating away from SAP BusinessObjects?

No. It runs on top of your existing SAP BusinessObjects Web Intelligence environment, universes and reports.

Does it require coding?

No. Features are configured visually through extension panels.
