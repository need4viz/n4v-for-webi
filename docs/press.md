## Decivision

Decivision is a French consulting firm specialized in decision-making and analytics solutions, and a Need4Viz integration partner. Their website includes the following references:

### Decivision — Need4Viz partner page
**URL:** https://www.decivision.com/decivision/partenaires/need4viz
Decivision presents Need4Viz on its dedicated partner page, positioning N4V FOR WEBI as a strategic extension for SAP BusinessObjects customers.

### Decivision blog — "Need4Viz Intelligence: AI at the service of SAP BusinessObjects"
**URL:** https://www.decivision.com/blog/sap-bi/need4viz-intelligence-l-ia-au-service-de-sap-businessobjects
Published January 28, 2026 by Salah-Eddine Hsina on the Decivision blog. Key points from the article:
- SAP BusinessObjects remains a robust and widely used platform but its native capabilities reach their limits on modern visualization, automated insight generation and suggested analyses.
- N4V Intelligence fills these gaps by adding augmented intelligence directly inside SAP BO, with no migration and no complex training required.
- Two features stand out in particular:
  - **Natural Language Query** — transforms user questions into executable BusinessObjects queries, making analysis accessible to more users.
  - **Chart Recommender** — automatically suggests the most suitable visual for the data and the analysis context.
- Together these features speed up ad-hoc analyses and improve the user experience inside SAP BO.
